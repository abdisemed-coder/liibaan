About Us

Liibaan General Trading is a leading national distributor of different products. Established in 1990 and headquartered in Hargeisa, the company has grown to become one of the largest independent wholesale distributors in Somaliland.

Team

Liibaan Wholesale is led by CEO Faisal Aden, who has over 40 years of experience in the wholesale distribution industry. The executive team also includes the CFO, COO, and heads of sales, procurement, and logistics
